How to Delete Your Account

  1. Go to your user profile by clicking on your User Image at the top right corner of the page.
  2. Scroll down to the "Delete Account" section.
  3. Click on the "Delete Your Account" button.
  4. Confirm your decision by clicking on the "Continue" button.
  5. Confirm Once again by clicking "Delete" button.

If you have any issues, please contact our support team for assistance.
